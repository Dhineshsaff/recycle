Quick Tips

Recycle Like a Pro

Small habits make a big difference. Keep these golden rules handy.

🚿

Rinse Before Tossing

Always rinse food and drink containers. Contaminated items can spoil an entire batch of recyclables.

🏷️

Leave Labels On

Paper and plastic labels can stay on bottles and jars — they are removed during the recycling process.

📦

Flatten Cardboard

Flatten boxes before placing them in the bin to save space and make collection more efficient.

🚫

No Plastic Bags

Loose plastic bags clog sorting machinery. Take them to dedicated bag-return points at supermarkets.

🔋

Battery Drop-off

Never put batteries in kerbside bins. Drop them at supermarkets, hardware stores or council depots.

💡

Wish-cycling Hurts

If you are unsure whether something is recyclable, check first. Wishful recycling contaminates loads.
